About us

18208859 Canada Inc. is a Canadian software company based in Whitby, Ontario. We build and run the MyMommy app.

MyMommy was founded by Akriti Saini after her own pregnancy and postpartum journey. She saw how much attention goes to the baby, and how little goes to the mother. MyMommy was built to change that.

Our mission

To help every mother feel informed, supported and a little less alone, at every stage, with clear and calm guidance she can trust. And to bring partners into the journey too, because motherhood is not a one person journey.

What MyMommy does

One app that grows with your family.

🌱 Trying to conceive

Period, temperature (BBT) and ovulation strip tracking, with fertile window insights and simple guides.

🤍 Pregnancy

Week by week guidance for you and your baby, symptoms explained, a kick counter, a contraction timer and labour preparation.

🌸 Postpartum, Mama Mode

Recovery after birth, week by week: healing, pelvic floor care, feeding and pumping support, and emotional wellbeing.

👶 Baby journey

Milestones, feeding, sleep, everyday care, safety and a vaccine tracker, from newborn to 4 years.

👨‍👩‍👧 Partner access

Your partner gets their own login and their own daily tips. View only, and you choose what they see.

MyMommy shares general health and parenting information. It does not diagnose and is not a replacement for your doctor, midwife or nurse. Always check with your healthcare provider about your own care.
