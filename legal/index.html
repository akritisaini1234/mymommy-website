2. Information we collect

A. Personal information

Sign-in: We offer Google and Apple sign-in. We receive identifiers needed to create and secure your account (such as name and email, depending on the provider). We use this only for authentication and account management.

When you accept our Terms and Privacy Policy, we save which version you accepted and the date and time, so we can show that you agreed.

Third-party services

We use trusted service providers only to run the app: Google for sign-in; Apple for sign-in, purchases and notification delivery (Apple push service); secure cloud hosting and database providers to store your account and journey data; and Anthropic (an AI content provider) to help generate some educational week-by-week tips. For AI tips we send only non-identifying context such as pregnancy week and pregnancy type, we do not send your personal health logs (cycle dates, BBT, LH results, notes, or photos) to that provider. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies; we recommend reviewing them for more details.

B. Health & journey inputs (you choose)

The information you enter about your cycle, pregnancy, baby and health is sensitive health information. We ask for your separate, specific consent to collect and use it, only to run the app for you (tracking, reminders, tips matched to your stage, and Partner Share if you choose it). It is for wellness and education only. It is not medical advice and we do not diagnose anything. You can withdraw this consent at any time in two ways: delete your account (Account, Delete My Account), or email hello@mymommy.ca. If you withdraw, we stop using the information, and the parts of the app that need it may no longer work. Examples include:

  • Fertility: cycle dates, symptoms, BBT/LH logs where you enter them. LH strip reading and ovulation estimates are for information only. They are not a medical device and not contraception.
  • Pregnancy: stage/week, symptoms, notes you save.
  • Baby: milestones and development notes you log.

Sensitive health data. Our specific commitment

Menstrual cycle, fertility, and reproductive health data is particularly sensitive. We treat it with the highest level of care:

  • We do not sell your reproductive health data under any circumstances.
  • We do not share this data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties, except where required by a valid legal order, or with processors who run the app for us (hosting, authentication, Apple billing verification).
  • Partner Share exception: if you invite a partner, journey stages you turn on for them become visible to that partner read-only. That is intentional sharing you control, not a sale.
  • You can delete all of your data at any time, including cycle logs, BBT readings, and LH strip results, by deleting your account (Account → Delete My Account).

C. Camera & photo library access

Features such as Flashback Journey (bump photo tracker) and LH strip analysis (ovulation tracking) ask for access to your camera and photo library. Here is exactly what happens with those photos:

  • Photos are never sent to MyMommy's servers. MyMommy does not upload, transmit, or store any photo on our servers or any third-party server. Ever. (Your phone's own backup, such as iCloud Backup, may include photos saved on your device, as it does for other apps.)
  • Bump photos taken with the camera are also saved to your phone's gallery (Camera Roll). MyMommy keeps a compressed copy for display in the app's private storage on your device. Never on our servers.
  • LH strip photos are read on your device to analyse the test line. The cropped strip picture is saved on your phone only so you can compare later. It is never uploaded. Only the numeric result (ratio, label, confidence) is saved to your account. If you take the photo with the camera, your phone saves the original to your gallery.
  • Deleting the app removes MyMommy's on-device records. Photos already saved to your gallery remain there under your full control.
  • Permissions: you can revoke camera or photo library access at any time in your device Settings → MyMommy → Permissions.

D. Technical & usage data

We may process basic technical data to operate the service, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. This includes security-related logs (such as your IP address), your notification push token (so reminders can reach your phone), your time zone (so reminders arrive at the right time), and simple counts of which groups of pages you visit. If Partner Share is on, your partner is also notified when you log a temperature or a strip. The app keeps your sign-in and settings on your phone and uses no advertising cookies or trackers. Optional in-app features (such as reminders) use your preferences and journey data only to schedule helpful prompts. Never to sell data.

Notifications: If you enable notifications, we may send you reminders, tips, and updates based on your stage, preferences, and activity within the app. On Premium, tips follow a daily morning / midday / evening pattern for your active stage. Free accounts get one short daily note, and Premium adds the full set of personalized notifications. Prep-only states (for example an expected birth date still in the future) do not receive lock-screen tips until that stage is an active cycle. By enabling notifications you agree to receive these push notifications and occasional in-app suggestions from us. They are not advertising from third parties. You can turn them off at any time in Account settings or in iPhone Settings, and you can withdraw this consent by turning them off.

Subscriptions & purchases: If you purchase Premium or Partner Share, Apple shares a transaction record with us so we can verify and activate your subscription. An Apple purchase is bound to one primary MyMommy login. We use that record to prevent the same paid plan from being used as multiple active primary accounts. We do not receive your payment card details. Those are handled entirely by Apple.

Partner Share: If you buy Partner Share and invite a partner, we store the partner email you enter, link status, and which journey stages you allow them to see. The partner can view those stages read-only when signed in with that email. We do not sell this data. Unlinking removes their access. It does not give you a second partner this year (see the Terms). Only enter an email for someone who has agreed to be invited.

Sharing with your partner: by default, your partner's notification switches copy yours. You can turn any of them off in Partner Share. If you would like to keep something private from your partner, please turn that stage off for them, or choose not to use Partner Share.
